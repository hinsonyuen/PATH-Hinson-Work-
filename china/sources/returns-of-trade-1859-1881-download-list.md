# Download list: Returns of Trade at the Treaty Ports, 1859–1881

From HathiTrust records 000505191 (Cornell, plus a few Princeton and Michigan parts), 008608736 (NYPL) and 001736476 (Michigan). That's 27 volumes in total.

**Why this is a list and not the files:** HathiTrust blocks downloads from cloud servers, so Claude couldn't fetch them. It turned away even single page images. Each volume needs to be downloaded from your own browser. Open the link, choose **Download → Whole item (PDF)**, and save it under the filename shown. Logging in with Berkeley (Log in → University of California, Berkeley) may be needed for whole-volume PDFs.
